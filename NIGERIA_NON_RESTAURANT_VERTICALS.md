# Nigeria Non-Restaurant Verticals for Dinee-Style Voice + Messaging Automation

## Overview
Dinee’s call-automation and order-intake capabilities can extend beyond restaurants to Nigerian businesses that receive high volumes of customer calls, require structured order/request capture, or need real-time status updates. Below are priority verticals with key use cases.

## 1) Pharmacies & Clinics
**Why it fits**: High call volume, repeat orders, urgent requests, and status updates.
- Prescription refills and medication availability checks.
- Appointment booking, reminders, and follow-ups.
- Delivery status updates (home delivery meds).

## 2) Grocery & Convenience (Supermarkets, Mini-Marts)
**Why it fits**: Similar to restaurant ordering, but with larger catalogs and repeat purchases.
- Phone/WhatsApp order intake and product availability checks.
- Substitution handling for out-of-stock items.
- Delivery scheduling and rider updates.

## 3) Retail & Electronics Shops
**Why it fits**: Call-heavy product inquiries and quote requests.
- Price checks, stock availability, and reservation/hold requests.
- Order confirmations and delivery coordination.
- Warranty and service follow-ups.

## 4) Logistics & Dispatch Services
**Why it fits**: Frequent inbound/outbound calls, real-time updates, and routing.
- Pickup and delivery booking by phone/WhatsApp.
- Dispatch confirmations, ETA updates, and proof-of-delivery.
- Customer support during delivery exceptions.

## 5) Ride-Hailing & Transport Operators
**Why it fits**: Booking calls, routing changes, and support workflows.
- Ride bookings and confirmations via voice/WhatsApp.
- Trip updates and driver assignment notifications.
- Customer support escalations and refunds.

## 6) Event Catering & Rentals
**Why it fits**: Quote-driven sales, scheduling, and follow-ups.
- Quote requests, menu selections, and availability checks.
- Booking confirmations and change management.
- Deposit/payment status updates.

## 7) Beauty & Personal Care (Salons, Spas, Barbers)
**Why it fits**: Appointment-heavy operations.
- Appointment booking, rescheduling, and reminders.
- Package upsell and service bundling.
- Follow-up and review requests.

## 8) Auto Services (Mechanics, Tire Shops, Car Wash)
**Why it fits**: Call-based scheduling and service updates.
- Appointment booking and service check-ins.
- Status updates during repair.
- Parts availability checks and approvals.

## 9) Real Estate Agencies
**Why it fits**: Lead qualification and appointment scheduling.
- Property inquiry intake and lead qualification.
- Viewing appointment scheduling.
- Updates on listings and availability.

## 10) Education & Training Centers
**Why it fits**: High inquiries and enrollment workflows.
- Course inquiries, lead capture, and follow-up.
- Enrollment appointments and reminders.
- Payment status confirmations.

## Selection Criteria (Quick Filter)
Prioritize verticals with:
- High inbound call volume and peak-hour bottlenecks.
- Structured requests that can be captured by voice/WhatsApp.
- Frequent status updates or confirmations.
- Clear ROI from reduced missed calls and faster response time.

## Suggested Pilot Targets in Nigeria
- Pharmacies with delivery services.
- Grocery delivery operators.
- Logistics/dispatch networks in major cities.
- Education centers with high lead volumes.
